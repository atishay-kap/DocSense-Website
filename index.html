Three steps, no setup.

There's no account to create and nothing to configure. Drop a file in and start asking.

01

Upload

PDF, Word, CSV, plain text, or Markdown - one file or several at once, side by side in the same workspace.

02

Ask

Type a question in plain language, or use a quick action to summarize, compare, or pull out key figures.

03

Get a grounded answer

Every answer is built from the document's actual text and tells you exactly which file it came from.

Built for reading, not just chatting.

Every feature exists to answer one question: does this actually help someone get through a document faster.

structured retrieval

Answers that keep their shape.

Ask for a comparison and get an actual table, not a paragraph pretending to be one. DocuGenie renders markdown properly - tables, bullet points, bold figures - so financial and structured data stays readable.

split-pane reading

The source, right next to the answer.

Open the document alongside the conversation and follow along as you ask. No switching tabs, no losing your place - the panel resizes, searches, and scrolls independently of the chat.

immersive mode

Read fullscreen, ask without leaving the page.

Switch into a fullscreen reader and keep a floating copilot on hand for anything that comes up mid-read, without ever losing your place in the document.

Every screen earns its place.

From upload to deep reading, the workspace stays calm while the useful details come forward.
